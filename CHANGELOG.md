## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-27

The first sprouts of Rootlings.

### Added
- Windows that know which way they look (by the phone's compass or by hand) and what's outside. The light on every sill is worked out hour by hour from the real sun and the real sky over your town (Open-Meteo, or MET Norway when Open-Meteo can't be reached), with real clouds, rain, snow and night in the panes.
- Catching with the phone's own camera. Pick from a catalogue of 133 houseplants, or describe a species of your own: look, type, light, watering, humidity, winter rest and flowers. The spirit takes the green of your plant's leaves from the photo.
- Soil without a watering calendar. Each pot dries at the pace of its species, its light and the dry air of the heating season. The spirit asks for a finger check, and every check teaches the estimate your home.
- Three forms for every spirit: new leaves, a monthly molt photo with before and after side by side, and an evolution when the plant has really grown.
- A nursery where cuttings are eggs that hatch with their ancestry. They can be given to friends as a text code.
- Battles against 12 real troubles, from spider mites to root rot and sunburn, fought with the real treatment, move by move. The repeat sprays unlock on their real days.
- A dex of every species: yours in colour, the ones seen at friends' or cafés in sepia, the rest as shadows. Also defeated pests, a herbarium for the plants that died, a note for whoever waters while you're away, and a morning reminder.
